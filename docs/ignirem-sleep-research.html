Baicalin

Human randomized evidence exists for baicalin in non-sleep indications. Sleep claims rely largely on preclinical literature; baicalein (the aglycone) is a related but chemically distinct molecule and is not substituted here.

Taurine

No robust human randomized trial was identified in which isolated taurine was tested specifically as a sleep intervention. Much of the sleep-related literature is observational, mechanistic, animal, or confounded by energy-drink combinations.
