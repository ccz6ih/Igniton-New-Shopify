N-Acetyl-L-Tyrosine (NALT)

Direct human clinical literature on the N-acetyl derivative itself is sparse. Most performance-under-stress trials use L-tyrosine, which should not be treated as equivalent evidence for NALT without pharmacokinetic justification. No direct human clinical study was selected for the curated layer because the exact-ingredient evidence is sparse or not clearly separable from related compounds/formulations.
